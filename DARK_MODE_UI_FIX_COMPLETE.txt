ROOT CAUSES OF BROKEN DARK MODE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Improper DarkColorScheme Definition
   ❌ onSurface = BackgroundLight (0xFFF4F6F9)
      - This is the light background color, not dark text
      - Creates white text on white cards - nearly invisible
   
   ❌ primary = CardSurfaceLight (0xFFFFFFFF)
      - Should be semantic primary accent
      - Not appropriate for dark theme
   
   ❌ Missing semantic dark mode status colors
      - No separate dark backgrounds for warnings/criticals
      - Bright warning/error colors on light backgrounds

2. Hardcoded Light-Mode Colors Throughout Screens
   ❌ BackgroundLight used as surface/component backgrounds
   ❌ CardSurfaceLight forced as text color
   ❌ Color.White with alpha for transparency
   ❌ PrimaryDark hardcoded in dark mode cards
   ❌ CharcoalText (dark) on dark surfaces
   ❌ CharcoalMuted too dim for secondary text in dark mode

3. Debug/Misleading Text in Visible UI
   ❌ "Vehicle Diagnostics — DEBUG: DiagnoseScreen Active"
   ❌ "✓ This is DiagnoseScreen" debug label
   ❌ "Real-Time Health Metrics" (not real-time)
   ❌ "OBD-II live telemetry from 18 vehicle sensors" (false)
   ❌ "Evaluated OBD-II Telemetry" (misleading)
